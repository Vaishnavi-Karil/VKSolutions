Common Inquiries

Answers to frequently asked questions about our engineering process and partnership model.

How do you handle project scalability?

expand_more
We design architectures with a "cloud-first" mindset, leveraging microservices and serverless components that automatically adjust to traffic demands. Our engineering standards ensure your product remains performant from 100 to 10 million users.

What is your typical engagement model?

expand_more
We offer both project-based fixed scope and dedicated team models. Most of our partners prefer the dedicated team approach, which allows for agile iteration and deep integration with your internal roadmap.

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?

expand_more
Absolutely. We provide Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for maintenance, security monitoring, and progressive feature development to ensure your software remains modern and bug-free.
